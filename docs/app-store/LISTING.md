Keywords follow Apple's rules: commas with no spaces, and no word that is
already in the name or subtitle (Choner, meet, move, healthy, habits,
together), since Apple indexes those anyway. "Jogging" was cut to fit the 100;
"running" covers most of those searches. "Friends", "social" and "dating" are
left out on purpose: they pull toward the dating reading the listing denies.

Promotional text is the one field that changes without a new version, so it
is where launch news goes later.
