Meet someone new. Build healthy habits together.

What if the person who helps you finally stick to healthy habits is someone you haven't met yet?

Choner is a new way to connect with people through healthy activities. Find someone who shares your interests, discover activities you can enjoy together and give each other a reason to show up.

Whether you want to start running, walk more, try yoga, cycle or work out at home, there's someone else who wants to do it too.

MEET PEOPLE WHO SHARE YOUR INTERESTS

You don't need to already have an activity partner. Tell Choner what you're interested in, what you want to work on and how you'd like to do it.

Choner introduces you to one potential activity partner at a time, based on your shared interests, preferences and location.

You can accept or pass and look for another match. No endless swiping, no profiles to browse and no strangers messaging you out of the blue. Just an opportunity to meet someone who wants to do the same healthy activities as you.

Already have someone in mind? Invite them to Choner instead.

FIND YOUR ACTIVITY PARTNER

Tell us what matters most to you right now:

- Move more
- Get stronger
- Reduce stress
- Improve your energy

Then choose an activity you would actually enjoy:

- Running and jogging
- Walking
- Cycling
- Yoga
- Home workouts

Choose how you want to do it and the areas you're comfortable meeting in. Choner uses your preferences to help find someone suitable.

CHOOSE HOW YOU SHOW UP

Together
Meet in person and enjoy the activity side by side. Find someone to run, walk or work out with.

Separately, together
Do the same activity in your own spaces, at your agreed times, while keeping each other accountable.

Two ways to show up. One shared commitment.

MAKE PLANS. SHOW UP. KEEP GOING.

Once you find someone, agree on what you want to do together and how often you want to do it.

Plan one session at a time. Agree on the day, time and meeting place when you're meeting in person. Nothing is confirmed until you both agree.

When the day arrives, let each other know when you're on your way. If you're meeting in person, use Choner's QR check-in to confirm you're both there.

After your activity, check in and let your partner know you showed up.

BUILD SOMETHING CONSISTENT TOGETHER

Your shared streak grows every time you both complete a commitment.

It's not about counting how many days you've exercised. It's about recognizing how many times you've shown up for each other.

Keep encouraging each other, celebrate the sessions you complete and make your next commitment together.

A LITTLE ABOUT SAFETY AND PRIVACY

Choner is for people aged 18 and over.

Choner is built around meeting people through shared healthy activities. It's about finding an activity partner, not a date.

You choose the area you're comfortable with. Choner does not need your exact location or live device location for in-person matching.

Your partner can see your first name, profile photo if you've added one, your shared activity, selected area and relevant participation updates. Your email address and private reasons for joining are not shared with your partner.

You can report or block a partner from within the app. Reports are handled by the Choner team.

Phone numbers, email addresses and external links are blocked in chat to help keep conversations within Choner.

WHERE CHONER WORKS

We're starting in Colombo, Sri Lanka, with in-person matching available in selected areas of Colombo and Greater Colombo.

Partner availability depends on your selected areas, preferences and the people currently looking for a match.
