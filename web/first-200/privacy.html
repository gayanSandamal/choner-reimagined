Privacy

What we do with what you tell us.

The First 200 · Last updated 14 October 2026

This page covers the form at join.choner.io, where you join The First 200 before Choner opens. If you later create an account in the app, the app has its own policy and it is the one that applies there.

The short version. We collect what we need to find you a good match and to tell you when we launch. We do not sell it, we do not advertise with it, and you can ask us to delete it at any time by emailing hello@choner.io.

Who we are

Choner, Sri Lanka. For anything on this page, write to hello@choner.io.

What the form collects

Everything below comes from answers you type or choose. There are no cookies, no trackers and no third-party analytics on this page.

  • Your name and email. Required, so we can tell you when we open.
  • Your phone number. Optional. Only used if email does not reach you.
  • Age range and gender. Used for matching, including matching same-gender only if that is what you choose in the app later.
  • Your area or district. So we can look for someone near you.
  • Your activity, and for workouts the types you pick.
  • When you prefer to meet — mornings, evenings and so on.
  • Whether you want to meet up or do it separately.
  • Your goal and what you struggle with — the two quiz answers.
  • Where you came from, if you arrived through someone's invite link or a campaign link.

Why we collect it, and on what basis

Two reasons, and nothing else. To match you with someone compatible before launch, and to contact you when Choner opens.

Under Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022, we rely on your consent, which is the box you tick before you finish the form. Matching you is also the service you asked us for by filling it in. You can withdraw consent at any time, and withdrawing it means we delete your entry.

What other people see

Your first name and your activity. Nothing else.

Not your email, not your phone number, not your exact area, not your age, not your quiz answers. Those are used to decide who to suggest, and are never shown to the person we suggest.

Who else handles it

  • Supabase — stores the data. Hosted in Singapore.
  • Resend — sends you the confirmation email and, later, the launch email.
  • Namecheap — hosts this page.

That is the complete list. We do not share your data with anyone else, and we never sell it.

How long we keep it

Until Choner launches and you have had a reasonable chance to use your access code, or until you ask us to delete it — whichever comes first. If you never use the code, we delete your entry within twelve months of launch.

Your rights

Under the PDPA you can ask us to:

  • Show you everything we hold about you.
  • Correct anything that is wrong.
  • Delete your entry entirely.
  • Stop emailing you, without deleting your place in the queue.

Email hello@choner.io and we will do it. We aim to reply within seven days. Every email we send also carries a one-click unsubscribe, and you can write to unsubscribe@choner.io at any time.

Security

The form is served over HTTPS and the data sits behind row-level security, which means the table cannot be read by anyone browsing the internet — only by our own server code. We are a small team and we are honest about the limit of that: no system is perfect, and we do not ask for anything we would not want to be responsible for. That is why we never ask for payment details, a password, or your ID.

Children

Choner is for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect anything from anyone younger. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

Changes

If we change this page we will update the date at the top. If a change meaningfully affects how we use what you have already given us, we will email you before it takes effect.
