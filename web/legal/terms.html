Who can use it

You need to be 18 or over, and the details you give us need to be true. We match people on what they tell us, so an inaccurate profile wastes somebody else's time as well as yours.

Your account

Keep your password to yourself. Anything done from your account is treated as done by you. If you think someone else has got in, email hello@choner.io and we will help.

What you owe your partner

This is the part that matters most, because there is a real person on the other side.

  • Show up, or say you cannot. Missing a session is human. Vanishing without a word is the one thing the whole product is built to prevent.
  • Be decent. No harassment, no abuse, no sexual advances, no pressure of any kind. A partner is not a date.
  • Meet safely. If you choose to meet in person, that is between the two of you. Pick a public place, tell someone where you are going, and use your judgement. Choner does not vet, interview or background-check anyone, and we are not present when you meet.
  • Keep what is shared, shared. Someone's photos, messages and reasons for starting are theirs. Do not post them anywhere else.

What you can post

Nothing illegal, nothing that harasses or threatens, nothing sexual, no spam, no advertising, and nothing that impersonates another person. You keep ownership of what you write and upload; you give us permission to show it to the people it was meant for.

Reporting someone

Every match has a report button. A report is read by a person on our team and it ends the match immediately. The person you report is never told who reported them or why. We would much rather you reported something small than talked yourself out of it.

When we suspend an account

We will suspend or remove an account that harasses people, repeatedly abandons partners without a word, posts content that breaks the rules above, or is used to break the law. Where we reasonably can, we will tell you why.

Leaving

Settings, then Delete account. No email required and no retention conversation. What happens to your data is set out in the privacy policy.

We will try to keep Choner working and your data safe. We cannot promise it is always available, that you will be matched, that a match will suit you, or that your partner will show up. They are a person, not a feature.
